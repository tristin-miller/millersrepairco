Questions

Is the cluster hard to remove?

No. Disconnect the battery, pull the trim panel, remove 4 screws and unplug it. About 15 minutes with basic hand tools.

Will my mileage change?

No. We repair your original cluster, so the odometer reading stays exactly the same and there's nothing to program.

How do I ship it?

Bubble wrap it in a sturdy box with 2 inches of padding on every side, and ship with tracking. No envelopes or padded mailers.

Can I drive while it's out?

It usually runs, but you'll have no gauges or warning lights. Best to park it until the cluster is back.

What if it can't be fixed?

We ask before doing any extra work. If we can't fix it, we ship it back and refund the repair fee.

What does the warranty cover?

Any part we replaced, for 1 year after we ship it back. If it fails, send it in and we fix it free.
