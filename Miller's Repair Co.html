What we fix

The gauges in these clusters are driven by small stepper motors that wear out with age. When they go, you see one or more of these.

  • Speedometer or tachometer stuck, jumping or reading wrong
  • Fuel, temperature, oil or voltage gauge dead or pegged
  • Needles that flutter or bounce while you drive
  • Needles that stay put after the key is off
  • Dim or dark odometer and gear display
  • Burned-out backlighting and indicator bulbs

Questions

      

      

        

          How do I remove the cluster?
          
Disconnect the battery, pull the trim panel around the cluster, remove the four screws that hold it in and unplug the connector on the back. On most of these trucks it takes about 15 minutes with basic hand tools.

        

        

          How should I pack it?
          
Wrap it in bubble wrap and put it in a sturdy box with at least two inches of padding on every side. Please don't ship it in an envelope or a padded mailer.

        

        

          Can I drive while the cluster is out?
          
The truck usually runs without it, but you'll have no speedometer, gauges or warning lights. Plan to park it until the cluster is back in.

        

        

          Will my mileage change?
          
No. We repair your own cluster, so the odometer keeps its reading. We don't change or program mileage.

        

        

          What if my cluster can't be repaired?
          
We contact you before doing any work you haven't approved. If we can't repair it, we ship it back and refund the repair fee.

        

        

          What does the warranty cover?
          
Any part we replaced, for one year from the day we ship it back. If something we fixed fails, send the cluster back and we repair it at no charge.

        

        

          Do you work on other vehicles?
          
We're starting with these GM trucks and SUVs and adding more older vehicles over time. Send a request with your year, make and model and we'll tell you if we can help.
